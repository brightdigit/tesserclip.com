TesserClip. Fix copied text before you paste it.

TesserClip opens straight to whatever you last copied, in a plain‑text editor. Edit it, copy it, and paste it where it belongs.

One window, one draft: the text you just copied, ready to fix.

Made for the moment between copy and paste.

TesserClip gives the text you just copied a clean place to be fixed. There are no files to save and no settings to learn.

01

Opens to your last copy

Open TesserClip and the text you just copied is already in a window, with the cursor at the end, ready to fix.

02

Every window is its own draft

Each window keeps the text it opened with. Copying something new never replaces it. Open the new text in another window with ⌘N.

03

A plain‑text editor

Rich text arrives as plain text. Smart quotes, dashes and other substitutions stay off until you turn them on in Edit ▸ Substitutions.

04

Copy All ⇧⌘C

Copy the whole window from the toolbar or the Edit menu in one step. Your selection and cursor stay where they were.

05

Asks before losing edits

Close a window with edits you haven’t copied and TesserClip asks first. If you’ve already copied them, it just closes.

06

Knows when there’s new text

A dot on New Window from Clipboard tells you there’s something new. TesserClip checks that it’s text without reading what it says.

Your edits are safe until they’re copied.

Close a window you’ve changed and TesserClip checks with you first. Cancel is one esc away, and nothing skips the question.

  • Quit with ⌘Q any time. Your windows and their text come back next time.
  • Already copied your edits? The window closes without asking.

No more pasting into TextEdit just to fix a typo.

Your text stays on your Mac.

TesserClip reads the clipboard only when it opens a window. Drafts are saved in the app’s own container on your Mac, so your windows come back after you quit.

Nothing leaves your Mac

Your text isn’t sent to BrightDigit or anyone else.

No account

There’s nothing to sign up for or sign in to.

No tracking

No analytics or ads in the app. Privacy Policy
