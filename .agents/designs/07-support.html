Common questions

Why is my new window empty? +−

The clipboard may not contain text, or TesserClip may not have permission to read it. Paste with ⌘VCommand V, or allow clipboard access.

What do I need to run TesserClip? +−

A Mac with macOS 27 or later.

How do I join the beta? +−

Install TestFlight from the Mac App Store, then open the TesserClip beta link on your Mac.

Where are my drafts stored? +−

On your Mac, inside TesserClip’s own container. They’re never uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.

Known issues in 1.0 beta 1

  • After you close a window, the clipboard text it opened with isn’t offered again as new text.
  • The empty‑window message comes back after you delete all of a window’s text.
  • If TesserClip can’t save your drafts, it doesn’t tell you yet.
